Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a service?

Simply search for the service you need, select a provider, choose a time, and confirm your booking.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, you can cancel up to 24 hours before the scheduled time without any charge.

How do I become a provider?

Sign up as a service provider and complete your profile. You'll need to provide necessary documents for verification.

Is my payment secure?

Yes, all payments are processed securely through our encrypted payment gateway.

What if I'm not satisfied?

You can contact support and we'll help resolve the issue. We have a satisfaction guarantee.

Do you offer refunds?

Refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis. Please contact support for assistance.
